About

Background

I'm a final-year Computing Systems student at Ulster University's London campus. Most of what I build sits at the boundary between hardware and the cloud — reading a signal, whether that's network traffic, a vibration, or an image upload, and deciding what to do about it. I care about the parts of engineering that are easy to skip: the on-device fault case, the persistence layer under load, the interlock that fires before something breaks.

PropGuard — Embedded TinyML Vibration Detector

COM683 · Coursework

A pre-flight safety check for drones: a frame-mounted accelerometer reads vibration, an on-device model classifies the fault in under 100ms, and a relay physically interlocks takeoff if something's wrong — trained on self-collected motor data, cross-checked against a published UAV vibration dataset.

Arduino Nano TinyML / Edge Impulse GY-521 (MPU-6050) Relay interlock
